Cookie Policy

How and why we use cookies on CookNextDoor.

📅 Last updated: 1 April 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember you, keep you logged in, and improve your experience. They are not programs and cannot carry viruses.

Essential cookies cannot be disabled — they are required for the platform to function. You can toggle optional cookies below.

Some cookies come from third-party services we use:

Browser controls

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from signing in. For instructions, visit your browser's help documentation.
