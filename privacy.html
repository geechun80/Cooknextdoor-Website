Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

📅 Last updated: 1 April 2025

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run the platform
  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time
  • We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage
  • Payments happen directly between users — we never touch your money

1. Who we are

CookNextDoor ("we", "us", "our") operates the website cooknextdoor.org and associated mobile interfaces. We are based in Singapore. For privacy queries, contact service@cooknextdoor.org.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: Name, email address, profile photo (via Google sign-in)
  • Cook listings: Dish name, description, price, availability, location (approximate)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, device type, browser
  • Communications: Messages sent via our Contact form

We do not collect payment details — all transactions occur directly between users.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your listings to nearby users
  • To improve the platform and fix bugs
  • To send service-related notifications (not marketing without consent)
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests

4. Data sharing

We share limited data with the following trusted services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication and database (Google LLC, USA)
  • Google Fonts / APIs: Font delivery and maps

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email service@cooknextdoor.org.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session and improve platform performance. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, Firebase security rules, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at service@cooknextdoor.org or via our Contact page.
