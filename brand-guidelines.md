# CookNextDoor Brand Guidelines

> Last updated: 2026-04-27

---

## Who We Are

CookNextDoor is a free hyperlocal platform where neighbourhood home cooks in Singapore sell extra food portions (3–5 servings) to nearby people within 1km. Zero commission, self-pickup, direct payment (PayNow, PayLah, cash). Community-first, not a delivery app.

---

## Tone of Voice

**Core personality**: Warm neighbour, not a corporate app.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your neighbor is cooking tonight" | "Order food now" |
| "Fresh, made with love" | "Fast delivery guaranteed" |
| "Join the community" | "Sign up for our platform" |
| "Pickup from just around the corner" | "Hyperlocal food marketplace" |
| Conversational, first-person ("we", "your") | Cold, transactional, formal |
| Emoji used warmly and sparingly | Emoji overload |

**Voice pillars**:
- **Human** — sounds like a person, not a startup
- **Local** — Singapore references, neighbourhood feel, familiar
- **Honest** — zero commission, no hidden fees, say it plainly
- **Warm** — food = care, not just a transaction

## Colour Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Lime / Brand accent** | `#E3EF26` | CTA labels, price tags, "New" badges, graphic overlays |
| **Deep forest / Primary dark** | `#06231D` | Text overlays on photos, background panels |
| **Teal / Secondary** | `#076653` | Supporting graphics, borders, icon backgrounds |
| **Cream / Light neutral** | `#F5F0E8` | Card backgrounds, light-mode photo frames |
| **WhatsApp green** | `#25D366` | WhatsApp CTA buttons and icons |

**Hero / OG image gradient:**
```css
background: radial-gradient(ellipse at 20% 40%, #0d3b2e 0%, #06231D 50%, #061a14 100%)
```

**Photo treatment guidelines:**
- Food photos: warm, natural light, home setting (not restaurant)
- Overlay text always uses `#E3EF26` (lime) on dark backgrounds, or `#06231D` on light
- Avoid pure white backgrounds — use `#F5F0E8` cream instead
- No stock-photo-looking images — real home kitchens preferred

---

## Logo Usage

- Min size: **115×72px** (never smaller)
- On dark backgrounds: use full-colour logo
- On lime `#E3EF26` background: use dark `#06231D` version
- Clear space: half the logo height on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or apply drop shadow
