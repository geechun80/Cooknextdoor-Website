# Workflow: Cook Guide Video — "How to List Your Food on CookNextDoor"

## Objective
Produce a step-by-step tutorial video (~2–3 minutes) showing home cooks exactly how to register, list their food, set a price, and receive payment. This video targets the cook side of the platform and builds trust with new cooks who are hesitant to sign up.

---

## Target Audience
- Home cooks (casual or active) in Singapore / Malaysia
- Age range: 25–60, likely female, cooking for family
- Motivations: earn extra income, reduce food waste, connect with neighbours
- Pain point: unsure if this is complicated or safe

---

## Required Inputs Before Starting
- Screen recording of the CookNextDoor.org cook registration flow
- Screen recording of the food listing form
- Brand colors and logo
- Example food photos (3–5 local dishes)

## Video Script Outline

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–10s)
**Visuals:** Home cook in kitchen, plating extra food portions.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "You cooked too much again — and that's a good thing. Let me show you how to share it with your neighbours and earn from it."

---

### Scene 2 — Why CookNextDoor? (10–25s)
**Visuals:** Animated text cards with key benefits.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "CookNextDoor is completely free. No commission, no delivery. You list, your neighbour picks up, and they pay you directly. It takes less than 5 minutes to get started."

---

### Scene 3 — Step 1: Sign Up (25–50s)
**Visuals:** Screen recording — opening cooknextdoor.org, clicking "I'm a Cook", filling in name and phone number.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Go to cooknextdoor.org and tap 'I'm a Cook'. Enter your name and phone number. That's all we need to get you started."

---

### Scene 4 — Step 2: List Your Food (50–90s)
**Visuals:** Screen recording — food listing form. Entering dish name, portions available, price, pickup time, location (neighbourhood only, not full address).
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Now list what you cooked. Add a photo of the dish — a phone photo is perfect. Set the number of portions, your price, and when it's ready for pickup. You don't need to share your full address — just your neighbourhood."

---

### Scene 5 — Step 3: Wait for a Buyer (90–120s)
**Visuals:** Phone notification appearing. Chat screen showing a buyer message.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Once you list, nearby buyers can see your food. When someone is interested, they'll message you directly. You agree on pickup time — and that's it."

---

### Scene 6 — Step 4: Collect Payment (120–150s)
**Visuals:** PayNow screen, PayLah logo, cash handover.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "When your neighbour picks up, they pay you directly — via PayNow, PayLah, or cash. No middleman. You keep 100% of what you earn."

---

### Scene 7 — Call to Action (150–180s)
**Visuals:** CookNextDoor logo on warm background. URL shown.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Ready to share your cooking with the neighbourhood? Sign up free at cooknextdoor.org. Your neighbours are already looking for what you make."

## Edge Cases
- If registration flow changes: re-record screen sections only, keep voiceover
- If cook is not tech-savvy: add on-screen pointer/highlight circles to guide eye
- If no screen recording tool: use Canva to recreate the UI steps as animated mockups
- Keep listing instructions accurate — do not show full home address at any point (privacy)
