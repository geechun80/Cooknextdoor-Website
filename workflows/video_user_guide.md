# Workflow: User Guide Video — "How to Find and Buy Home-Cooked Food Near You"

## Objective
Produce a step-by-step tutorial video (~2–3 minutes) showing consumers (buyers) how to discover nearby home-cooked food on CookNextDoor, contact a cook, arrange pickup, and pay directly. This video targets the buyer side of the platform and lowers the barrier for first-time users.

---

## Target Audience
- Nearby residents looking for affordable, real home-cooked meals
- Age range: 18–50, working adults, students, families
- Motivations: save money vs. food delivery, eat healthier, support neighbours
- Pain point: don't know how to find listings or if it's trustworthy

---

## Required Inputs Before Starting
- Screen recording of CookNextDoor.org consumer browsing experience
- Screen recording of a food listing page (example listing)
- Screen recording of the contact/chat flow
- Brand colors and logo
- Example photos of dishes available on the platform

## Video Script Outline

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–10s)
**Visuals:** Person on phone, looking at expensive food delivery prices. Sighs. Then opens CookNextDoor — sees affordable home-cooked listings nearby.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Tired of $8 delivery fees for a $6 meal? Your neighbour just cooked curry — and it's only $4 for two portions."

---

### Scene 2 — What You Get (10–25s)
**Visuals:** Animated text cards. Real food photos.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "CookNextDoor shows you real home-cooked food from cooks within 1km of you. No delivery. No platform fees. You pick it up and pay the cook directly."

---

### Scene 3 — Step 1: Browse Nearby Listings (25–55s)
**Visuals:** Screen recording — opening cooknextdoor.org, scrolling listings filtered by neighbourhood. Food photos, dish names, prices, portions, pickup times visible.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Go to cooknextdoor.org and browse food listings near you. You'll see the dish, how many portions are left, the price, and when it's ready for pickup. No account needed just to browse."

---

### Scene 4 — Step 2: Contact the Cook (55–90s)
**Visuals:** Screen recording — tapping a listing, seeing the cook's first name and neighbourhood, tapping the contact button, sending a message.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Found something you like? Tap to see more details, then contact the cook directly. Introduce yourself and confirm the pickup time. It's that simple — like messaging a neighbour."

---

### Scene 5 — Step 3: Pick Up Your Food (90–120s)
**Visuals:** Person walking short distance. Cook at door handing over food container. Friendly interaction.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "At the agreed time, head over and pick up your food. No waiting for a rider. No cold meals. Just fresh, home-cooked food from someone nearby."

---

### Scene 6 — Step 4: Pay Directly (120–145s)
**Visuals:** PayNow screen, PayLah logo, cash.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Pay the cook directly via PayNow, PayLah, or cash. No hidden charges. No platform commission. What you see is what you pay."

---

### Scene 7 — Trust & Safety Note (145–165s)
**Visuals:** Text card with reassurance points.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Every cook is a real person from your neighbourhood. You see their first name, their area, and reviews from other buyers. It's community — not a faceless app."

---

### Scene 8 — Call to Action (165–180s)
**Visuals:** CookNextDoor logo + URL.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Ready to eat better for less? Visit cooknextdoor.org and find what's cooking near you — right now."

## Edge Cases
- If no listings exist in the demo area during recording: use a pre-seeded test account with sample listings
- If contact/chat flow is not yet live: record the intended UX as a mockup in Canva or Figma
- If user is watching without sound: ensure every spoken word appears as an on-screen caption
- Keep cook's full address off-screen at all times (privacy rule)
