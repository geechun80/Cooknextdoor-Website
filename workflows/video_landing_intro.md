# Workflow: Landing Page Intro Video — "What is CookNextDoor?"

## Objective
Produce a short (~60–90 second) platform introduction video to embed on the CookNextDoor.org landing page hero section. This video explains who CookNextDoor is for, what problem it solves, and why it is different from food delivery apps.

---

## Target Audience
- First-time visitors to the landing page (both cooks and consumers)
- Age range: 20–55, Singapore/Malaysia residents
- Viewing context: mobile phone, likely muted — captions required

---

## Required Inputs Before Starting
- Access to CookNextDoor.org website for visual reference
- Brand colors: warm tones (orange, cream, soft brown)
- Logo file
- Any existing testimonial quotes from cooks or users (optional)

---

## NotebookLM Research Queries
*(Follow Pattern 11: Effective Prompt Guidelines from usage_patterns.md)*

Use these prompts if you have a CookNextDoor document or brief loaded in NotebookLM:

**Query 1 — Core Messaging (Source-Scoped)**
> "Based only on the CookNextDoor project brief, what is the single most important problem this platform solves for home cooks and for food buyers?"

**Query 2 — Differentiators (Extraction Task)**
> "List all the ways CookNextDoor is different from food delivery platforms like GrabFood or Foodpanda. Use only what the document states."

**Query 3 — Tone Refinement (Content Refinement)**
> "Rewrite the platform description in a warm, community-driven tone suitable for a 90-second explainer video. Avoid corporate language."

**Query 4 — Output Format**
> "Give me a bullet-point list of the 5 most important facts a first-time visitor needs to know about CookNextDoor in under 30 seconds of viewing."

---

## Video Script Outline

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–10s)
**Visuals:** Close-up of home-cooked food being portioned. Steaming rice, curry, or local dishes.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Your neighbour is cooking extra food today. Wouldn't it be nice to just grab a plate?"

---

### Scene 2 — Problem (10–25s)
**Visuals:** Split screen — a cook throwing away leftover food / a person scrolling food delivery apps and seeing high prices.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Home cooks make too much. Neighbours want affordable, real food. But there was no easy way to connect — until now."

---

### Scene 3 — Solution (25–50s)
**Visuals:** Phone screen showing CookNextDoor.org. Cook listing a meal. Nearby buyer seeing the listing. Simple chat, pickup, payment.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "CookNextDoor is a free platform where home cooks in your neighbourhood can share extra portions — and you can pick them up directly. No delivery fees. No commission. Just real food from real people, 1km away."

---

### Scene 4 — How It Works (50–70s)
**Visuals:** 3 animated steps (can use simple text cards):
1. Cook lists their extra food
2. Neighbour finds it nearby
3. Pick up and pay directly (PayNow / PayLah / cash)

**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Cooks list what they made. You find it nearby. Pick it up. Pay directly. That's it."

---

### Scene 5 — Call to Action (70–90s)
**Visuals:** CookNextDoor logo + website URL on warm background.
**Voiceover / Caption:**
> "Less waste. More connection. Better meals. Join CookNextDoor today at cooknextdoor.org — it's completely free."

## Edge Cases
- If no voiceover: use bold on-screen text cards with background music only
- If video is too long: cut Scene 2 (problem) and go straight from hook to solution
- If embedding on landing page: keep file under 10MB, use MP4 H.264, autoplay muted with captions
