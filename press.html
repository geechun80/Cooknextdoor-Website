Fast facts

🏠

What is CookNextDoor?

A free hyperlocal platform connecting home cooks with hungry neighbours within 1 km. Zero commission. No delivery. Direct payment.

📍

Where do we operate?

Singapore and Malaysia. Expanding to more Southeast Asian cities in 2025–2026.

💸

Business model

Completely free for cooks and buyers. No commission, no subscription fee. We grow through community trust.

🌱

Mission

Reduce food waste, build neighbourhood communities, and help home cooks earn from their passion.

📅

Founded

2024, Singapore. Bootstrapped and community-funded.

👥

Team size

Small, passionate team of builders and food lovers based in Singapore.
